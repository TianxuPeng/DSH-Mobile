OFFICIAL QR ANALYSIS
Source: https://github.com/saya-ch/dsh-mobile (Apache-2.0)
The official Android app accepts HTTPS pairing links:

  https://<origin>/mobile-access/pair#key=dsh2.<64 hex instanceId>.<43 token>
  https://<origin>/mobile-access/pair#instance=<64 hex>&token=<43 token>

It then:
- GET /mobile-access/ca.cer and validates the CA fingerprint == instanceId
- POST /mobile-access/auth/native-pair {token,label} with CA-pinned TLS
- reads sessionToken/csrfToken/deviceToken
- installs dsh_ma_session, dsh_ma_csrf and __Host- aliases
- opens the origin with those cookies
- renews via POST /mobile-access/auth/native-renew {deviceToken}

IMPLEMENTATION IN dshMobile 1.2.0
- PairingLink parses both current and legacy fragments; rejects HTTP, query strings,
  wrong paths and malformed keys.
- PairingActivity performs CA fetch/fingerprint validation, native-pair, encrypted
  PairingStore save, session-cookie installation and opens the paired origin.
- BrowserActivity recognizes the paired origin (including the bare origin root),
  renews the session when reconnecting, and enables the balance bar for it.
- WebView and WSS SSL errors are bypassed only for the exact paired Origin,
  SslError.SSL_UNTRUSTED, and a leaf certificate directly signed by the pinned CA
  with a matching SAN.
- PairingStore encrypts the device token/CA with an Android Keystore AES-GCM key.
- Scanner shows "继续连接上次电脑" when a record exists.
- One paired computer is stored; official multi-device list and iroh are not implemented.
